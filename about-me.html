About Me

Hey, I'm Prince. Second-year CSE (AI & ML) student, currently being slow-cooked between university assignments and the parallel life I'm building on YouTube tutorials at 1x speed pretending it's 2x. My days go something like: attend class, understand 40% of it, go home, teach myself the other 60% through a random blog written by someone who clearly forgot to explain the important part. Repeat. Cry a little. Repeat again. I build things in the gaps my syllabus doesn't fill. Which, coincidentally, is most of the syllabus. My professors think I'm learning what they're teaching. My browser history says otherwise. I've made peace with being perpetually behind on my curriculum and perpetually ahead on things nobody asked me to learn. Balance, I've decided, is a myth invented by people who don't have both a mid-sem exam and a broken deployment due the same week. Not sure if this is what "growth" is supposed to look like, but I'm doing it anyway, one existential crisis and one working prototype at a time.

Built by a human who Googled "how to center a div" more times than he'd like to admit.
